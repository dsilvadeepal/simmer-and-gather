These groups overlap. “No explicit listed ingredient matched” is **not** a gluten-free or nut-free claim. Verify recipe variants, optional ingredients, packaged products, substitutions and cross-contact before serving someone with an allergy. Coconut is not automatically classified as a tree nut here.

The new Christmas fruit cake lists all-purpose flour, almonds and walnuts. Black Chana Sukka has no explicit listed gluten or nut ingredient, but the packaged bafat powder needs label review. For the Diwali shortlist, Aloo Chaat in Phyllo Shells lists phyllo; Vegetable Pulao lists cashews; and Carrot Halwa lists almond ingredients.

This is ingredient screening for planning, not a medical assessment or certification of a prepared dish.
