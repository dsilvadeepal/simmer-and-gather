These groups overlap. “No explicit source ingredient matched” is **not** a gluten-free or nut-free claim. Recipe variants, optional ingredients, packaged sauces/spices, substitutions and cross-contact need checking before serving a guest with an allergy. A source ingredient such as naan, pie crust or crescent dough is flagged because its usual form contains wheat; verify the exact product. Coconut is not automatically classified as a tree nut here.
