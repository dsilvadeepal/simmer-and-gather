## Basis and limits

- Portions marked **chef estimate** are approximate and may represent starter pieces, dessert slices or condiment spoonfuls rather than full meal servings. Confirm the actual party portion before scaling to 15 guests.
- Equipment lists name likely appliances and cold storage, not their capacities. Air-fryer batches, oven shelf space, pot size and simultaneous use remain gathering-specific.
- Prep proposals use short, practical windows (usually the previous day). Cool perishable cooked food promptly in shallow containers, refrigerate within two hours, and reheat leftovers to 165°F where relevant. [FoodSafety.gov food handling](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety), [safe temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).
- The listed turkey is 14–16 lb. A frozen 12–16 lb turkey needs about 3–4 days to thaw in a refrigerator; oven scheduling must also account for the measured cooking endpoint. [FoodSafety.gov turkey chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/meat-poultry-charts).
- Gluten/nut screening is ingredient-level; exact brands and cross-contact cannot be decided from a recipe page.
