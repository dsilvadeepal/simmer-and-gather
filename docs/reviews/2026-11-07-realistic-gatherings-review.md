# Twenty realistic gatherings with a free calendar

November 7, 2026. Uses 32 recipes from a read-only snapshot of the current 40-recipe local library. No live calendar access. Two unreviewed library entries were not selected. All cases use a solo host, a standard home kitchen, no known allergies, full recipe-yield portions for each guest, and the existing single-batch kitchen setting. This is a scheduler evaluation, not physical cooking validation or a complete allergy/helper test suite.

19/20 original menus complete after corrections. The unchanged Indian buffet requires aloo chaat assembly within 45 minutes of serving, conflicting with its 60-minute no-work buffer. A separately tested swap to air-fryer paneer tikka completes with the same 18 guests, three 180-minute prep days and 60-minute buffer. This alternative does not replace the original test.

## What changed

- Seven saved recipe records corrected: vegetarian metadata on paneer makhani, chana masala, pulao, rice pudding, dal makhani and carrot halwa; pressure/cooling attention corrected for pudding and chana, sauce attention for paneer; sweet potato pie make-ahead and passive-time split corrected.
- All elapsed pressure-cooking and baking durations retained. Attendance estimates include setup/return points. Pie cooling and optional cream split explicitly; refrigerator storage retained.
- Rice pudding uses a chilled make-ahead route, with a 24-hour planning allowance. Sweet potato pie uses a source-supported 48-hour make-ahead route.
- Cleanup remains a full 15-minute host task. It may occupy a passive cooking gap after work has begun, and cannot overlap active work, exceed the daily cleanup allowance or erode the buffer.
- All non-recipe application records were fingerprinted before/after applying the seven recipe updates and were unchanged. No Notion or calendar writes.

## What you need to change

Nothing for the 19 complete scenarios. For the Indian buffet, choose the tested paneer tikka starter instead of aloo chaat. Alternatively, explicitly allow its final assembly within the last 45 minutes; this is a preference change, not applied here. More prep time or another prep day does not solve that freshness/buffer conflict.
